# Jet Dodge

Free single-file HTML5 arcade game. Hold to thrust, release to fall — guide a
jetpack pilot through an endless night-city gauntlet while dodging laser
walls, rotating laser turrets and tracking missiles. Graze hazards for NEAR
MISS bonuses, grab a shield every 800 points, ride the slow-motion near-miss
replay and chase your best score.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/jet-dodge/

- Hold anywhere (touch) / hold mouse / hold Space to thrust, release to fall
- Dual scoring: distance altitude (10 m = 1 pt) + near miss (+2 pts), slow motion 0.2 s
- Hazards: gapped laser walls, rotating turrets (2/4 beams), homing missiles
  that track your predicted heading for 4 s then fly straight
- Shield powerup every 800 points blocks one hit; best score saved locally
- Procedural WebAudio sounds (jet noise, laser hum, missile alarm, near-miss
  chime), parallax city silhouettes, particles — zero external assets
- Single index.html, no dependencies, no tracking, works offline once loaded
